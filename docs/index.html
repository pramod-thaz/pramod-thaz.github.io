I

Institutional Probabilistic Analytics & Risk Consulting

Rigorous, uncertainty-aware frameworks for institutions that need decisions to survive a risk committee. Three solution modules, one for each sub-audience.

Allocators & pension funds

Multi-asset risk-budgeting & structural allocation under uncertainty

Problem
Traditional mean-variance optimization breaks out-of-sample due to extreme sensitivity to expected-return inputs.
Deliverable
Custom hierarchical Bayesian shrinkage & Black-Litterman Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) models.
Value
Absorbs subjective market views, buffers against tail risk, and stabilizes asset weights across changing macro regimes without over-concentrating positions.

Quant funds & systematic alpha

Causal discovery & regime-switching market mechanics

Problem
Linear backtests overfit static historical distributions and break down during structural regime shifts.
Deliverable
PyMC/JAX-driven Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) with Student-t emission distributions and causal d-separation frameworks.
Value
Isolates true causal alpha drivers from latent market noise and enables dynamic real-time risk / volatility targeting.

Risk teams & LPs / GPs

Probabilistic performance attribution & style-drift identification

Problem
Retrospective Brinson-Fachler models fail to separate true manager selection skill from accidental factor / sector tilts.
Deliverable
Latent-variable hierarchical attribution frameworks.
Value
Decouples alpha, selection skill, and market friction into dynamic latent states — flagging hidden style drift before it compromises fund performance.

Meridian — the Track 3 product — began as this practice's proof-of-concept for Bayesian un-smoothing: P(Vreal | Mpublic) ∝ P(Mpublic | Vreal) · P(Vappraisal), reconciling real-time public-market signals with smoothed private appraisals across mixed public/private portfolios.
