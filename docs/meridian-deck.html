The Problem

The Allocator's Dilemma

Structural risk trapped in private assets — data sparsity, appraisal lag, and fragmented analytics leave allocators blind to true portfolio risk.

  1. The Appraisal Smoothing Illusion

    Quarterly appraisals lag public markets by 3–6 months, artificially dampening reported volatility and inflating Sharpe ratios — masking real systemic tail risk.

  2. Unstructured Data Silos

    Critical covenant terms, debt maturities, and tenant escalation clauses remain trapped in 50+ page PDF deal memos and GP reports.

  3. Fragmented Analytics

    Liquid holdings tracked in real-time portfolio software while private deals live in isolated analyst DCF workbooks — leaving allocators blind to true correlations.

Quantitative Engine

Bayesian Un-Smoothing

Instead of treating quarterly appraisals as static ground truth, Meridian treats them as prior probability distributions updated continuously against live public market signals.

P(Vreal | Mpublic)  ∝  P(Mpublic | Vreal) · P(Vappraisal)
Posterior valuation given market signals  =  likelihood × prior appraisal distribution
